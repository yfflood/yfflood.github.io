Yifan Hong

I study decision-making with an ambition to connect normative theories with descriptive models.

I am a PhD student in industrial engineering at Tsinghua University and currently visiting Prof. Huiwen Jia’s lab at UC Berkeley. I received my B.E. in industrial engineering from Tsinghua University in 2023.
